Chapter 3.6: Snow or Rain

What you are learning

Assume temperature stores a Fahrenheit temperature from 0 to 212. Write an if/else statement. If temperature is less than or equal to 32, assign 'Snow' to weather; otherwise assign 'Rain'.

Example

temperature = 28 if temperature <= 32: weather = 'Snow' else: weather = 'Rain'
