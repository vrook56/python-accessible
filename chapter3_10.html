Chapter 3.10: Conditional Expression

What you are learning

Assume number1 and number2 are integers. In one print statement using an f-string, use a conditional expression to display both values and which is larger.

Example

a = 4 b = 9 print(f'The larger of {a} and {b} is {a if a > b else b}')
